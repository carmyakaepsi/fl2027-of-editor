## FL2027 OF Editor By Epsi — Alpha 0.97

**Download:** `FL2027_OF_Editor_By_Epsi_Alpha_0.97.zip` below (exe + README + CHANGELOG). Keep a copy of your EDIT / ML / LG files.

### New in 0.97
- **League mode** (new tab, experimental): open `LG00000000` — standings of every league, last results and next matchday, move players between clubs, bring squads (and game plans) in line with your option file, edit game plans, simulate the rest of the season from the real table.
- **Master League transfers solved**: a club can hold at most **40 players under contract, loans included**. Moves into a full club never registered (the player ended up a free agent). The editor now shows *squad + on loan = total/40* and blocks those moves.
- **Scout DB: about 126,000 players** (Czech Republic, Austria, Ukraine, Norway, Sweden added).
- Match Sim: saved line-ups that cannot be right (several goalkeepers, many players out of position) are replaced by the best available eleven.
- FL2026 Master League saves open too.

### Since 0.8 (0.8.1 – 0.96)
- **Scout DB** grown from 15,700 to 126,000 players (England, Spain, France, Germany with their second divisions, Netherlands, Belgium, Portugal, Turkey, Scotland, Denmark, Greece, Russia, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, MLS, Japan, Saudi Arabia, China, Brazil and more), with **matching on age, club, role and nationality first**, a screen to resolve doubtful matches one by one, and **Search Scout DB** on the player page.
- **Match Sim**: **League season** (whole season, standings, scorers, injuries and suspensions, derbies, 100-season odds), **Matchday** (your own list of matches), **most likely scorers**, form modes, engine recalibrated on whole seasons, stands and two-click line-ups.
- **Game Plan and player page**: two clicks to swap any two players; clickable position map (A / B / C); second strikers from the Scout DB.
- **Managers**: three new attributes (man management, tactical knowledge, youth development); choosing a manager who leads another team swaps the two (a team without a manager crashes the game).
- **Master League**: game plans of every club, career dates and next match, line-ups kept consistent after moves.
- The player page closes with a click outside it.
